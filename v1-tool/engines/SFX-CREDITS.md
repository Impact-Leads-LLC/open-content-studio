# Sound effect credits

The sound effects in the engines' `assets/sfx/` folders (whoosh, whoosh-short, click, click-soft, pop, impact-bass-1,
impact-bass-2) come from the HyperFrames sound library (`skills/media-use/audio/assets/sfx`, Apache 2.0), which sources
them from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free for commercial and non-commercial use,
modification and redistribution as part of derivative works, no attribution required.
