# Voice Guardrails

The banned phrases and framings. Every writer and the coach sweep every draft against this list before anyone sees
it. Add to it every time a draft makes you wince; the newest additions matter most.

## Banned phrases and framings

Starters that work for most people:

- Contrast-correction constructions in any form ("it's not X, it's Y", "X, never Y" as a correction).
- Punchy fragment stacks that label instead of explain ("Three areas. One bottleneck. Find yours.").
- Em dashes. Use a comma, a period or a new sentence.
- Manufactured sales lines and fake reassurance ("If I can't help, I'll tell you on the call.").
- Invented casual filler ("Here's the thing.", "Let that sink in.", "Real talk:").
- Filler words: "actually", "real" (as in "real results"), "game-changer", "unlock".
- [Your own: words, metaphors or claims you never want written in your name]
