## The five awareness levels

Every piece is built for one reader. Much of the audience starts at the top, so the board holds pieces for every level.

- **Unaware** (top of funnel): doesn't know [the idea at the heart of the main offer] is an answer. Open the idea with a
  story, a surprising result, or the idea itself.
- **Problem-aware** (top): feels [the ideal client's main pain, in their words] but doesn't see the way out. Name the
  problem, show what it costs, hint at the answer.
- **Solution-aware** (middle): knows the approach exists, often with a wrong idea of what it's for. Show how it works:
  the mechanism, the mistakes, comparisons, numbers.
- **Product-aware** (bottom): knows the owner and the offer but isn't sure it's for them. Proof and trust: client
  results, how the work goes, objections answered.
- **Most aware** (bottom): close to booking. The reason to act now: what the free call gets them, what happens on it, a
  client result, the next step made easy.

## Angles

One core insight is told several ways, each its own piece with its own hook: an unaware story, a problem-aware wake-up,
a solution-aware how-to, a product-aware proof, a most-aware reason to book now.

## The highest intention

People save it to use later: steps they can follow, a script they can copy, a checklist, numbers they can use. When two
ideas are close, the one people would save wins.
