Send the first invoice the day the work starts.

Most studios wait until the project ends. On net 30, with a client who pays a little late, that's about 60 days before the money shows up.

Here's the three-payment split I teach:

- A third when the work starts
- A third at the midpoint
- A third on delivery

Three steps to put it in place:

1. Write the split into the price section of every proposal, before anyone signs.
2. Send the first invoice with the signed proposal.
3. Put the midpoint invoice on your calendar the day the project starts.

Priya runs a video studio. She used to wait about 60 days on every project. Now new projects pay the first third within a week of kickoff.
