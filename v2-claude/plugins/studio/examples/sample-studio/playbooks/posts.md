# Posts: the kinds of text post, and when to use each

A text post is one idea told well in writing, for one reader, with something worth saving. The studio picks a type by
the reader's level and keeps the week varied.

## The types

| Type | Shape | Length | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short hook post | 1 to 3 short lines, one sharp idea | under 35 words | none, or the full episode |
| Teaching | the hook, the common belief, the better way, the reasons, one takeaway | 120 to 280 words | save, and a light step |
| How-to | the hook, numbered steps, where it stops working | 150 to 300 words | save, and a lead magnet |
| Story and lesson | a time-stamped open, the turn, the lesson said plainly, the bridge to the reader | 200 to 400 words | save, and one step |
| Client result | how the client went from before to after: the problem, what changed, the result, what you respect about them | 150 to 300 words | your call |
| Objection | the doubt in the reader's words, the honest answer, the proof | 120 to 250 words | your call |
| Invitation | the problem the offer solves, what they get (concretely), one date or one link | 80 to 200 words | one ask |

## Which type for which reader

- Unaware: story posts and short hook posts.
- Problem-aware: teaching posts that name the problem and what it costs.
- Solution-aware: how-tos and comparisons.
- Product-aware: client results and objections.
- Most aware: invitations, with the offer made plain and the next step made easy.

## What makes a post work

- The first line is a door, never a summary (`hooks.md`).
- One idea per line, white space between, short paragraphs: easy to read on a phone.
- Specific numbers from your material, with what they depend on ("for a 10-person team").
- Something to keep: steps, a script, a checklist, numbers.
- Your way of putting things, from `voice.md`. If you wouldn't say it out loud, it goes.
- A plain save line just before the end ("Save this for your next proposal."), then one next step, last.
- Lists of three as dash bullets only when they're real lists; steps numbered.
- A client is named only the way they agreed to be named.
