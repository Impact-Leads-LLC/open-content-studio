# Voice: Dana Lee

## How I sound
- Short sentences. Owner to owner.
- Calm about money, never alarmed.
- A dry joke now and then, never at the client's expense.
- Specific: a number, a day, a document.

## Reading level
Fifth grade: short words, one idea per sentence, contractions.

## Words and phrases I use
- "cash in view"
- "the month swings"
- "payroll Friday"

## Words and phrases I never use
- "financial freedom"
- "money mindset"

## Formatting
- One idea per line, white space between.
- No emoji. No bold.

## Lines that are mine, used word for word
- "Send the invoice the day the work starts."
