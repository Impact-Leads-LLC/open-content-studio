## My ideal client
- Who they are: the owner of a small creative studio, great at the work, doing the books at night.
- What they say about the problem:
  - "We're busy and still broke at the end of the month."
  - "I check the bank balance before I check email."
- What they want:
  - "I want to know payroll is covered without doing math at midnight."
- What they believe now: more clients will fix the cash problem.
- What they've tried: a bookkeeper, a line of credit, chasing invoices harder.

## The idea my market doesn't know yet

- Invoice day one: the first invoice goes out the day the project starts.
- The three accounts: income, owner pay, taxes.
