Most studios invoice when the project ends. On net 30 terms, and with clients who pay a little late, that's about 60
days between doing the work and seeing the money.

That gap is a loan. You're paying your team for two months of work your client hasn't paid for yet.

The fix is the three-payment split. A third when the project starts, a third at the midpoint, a third on delivery.

Send the invoice the day the work starts. Not the day after the kickoff call. The day the work starts.

Step one: put the split in every proposal, in the price section, before anyone signs.
Step two: send the first invoice with the signed proposal.
Step three: put the midpoint invoice on the calendar the day the project starts.
