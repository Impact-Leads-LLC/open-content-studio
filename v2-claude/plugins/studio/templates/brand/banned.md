# Banned phrases and constructions

The lint (lint.mjs) checks every line under "Phrases" and "Patterns" in every post, caption and slide, and the coach's
lint check rests on it. Add the member's own bans under "Phrases", one per line. A guest's quote stays word for word
even when it holds a banned word: the bans apply to what we write around it.

## Patterns
- /\b(it'?s|this is)\s+not\s+(just\s+)?about\b/ contrast opener ("it's not about X")
- /\b(only|just)\s+\d+\s+spots?\b/ invented scarcity
- /\b(6|7|six|seven)[- ]figure\b/ income-bracket hype

## Constructions to rewrite (the coach looks for these; a script can't catch them all)
- Contrast corrections in any form: "it's not X, it's Y", "X isn't the problem, Y is", "less X, more Y".
- Stacks of two- and three-word fragments ("No pitch. No pressure. Just results.").
- Rhetorical questions that open a post when the answer is known, and yes/no question openers ("Are you...?").
- "Imagine..." openers.
- Narrator lines about the reader's situation ("You've been there.").
- Rule-of-three lists used for rhythm instead of meaning.
- Invented urgency, income promises, or "DM me" unless that is the member's real next step.
