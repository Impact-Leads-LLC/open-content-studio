# Open Content Studio

**Free from [Mike Moll](https://www.mikemoll.co/?utm_source=open-content-studio&utm_medium=github) · mikemoll.co ·
Helping service founders get on podcasts and generate revenue.**

A free content studio that turns your episodes, videos and teaching material into reels, carousels, posts and
graphics, written in your voice and reviewed before anything goes out. Two ways to run it:

| | Version 1: the full tool | Version 2: Claude only |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A web app your team signs in to: an episode board, a review queue, a posting calendar and publishing to your scheduler | A Claude plugin: drop a video or transcript into Claude and it makes, edits and reviews everything in the chat |
| Runs on | Your own Supabase project and Vercel account | The Claude desktop app (Cowork or Code) |
| Best for | A team that posts several pieces a day and reviews them together | One person, or a small team, who wants no extra tool |
| Setup | About an hour, guided by Claude Code (`v1-tool/`) | About 20 minutes, guided by the plugin (`v2-claude/`) |

Start with the two-page setup guide, [`docs/Open_Content_Studio_Setup_Guide.pdf`](docs/Open_Content_Studio_Setup_Guide.pdf):
the tools you need, how they connect, every key and what it does, and the steps. Then open the version you want. Each
folder has its own README and a guided setup that interviews you, collects your brand material and builds everything
with you.

**Downloads:** the latest [release](../../releases/latest) has a zip of each version, a ready-to-upload `studio.zip`
for Cowork, and the setup guide.

**Version 2 in Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add Impact-Leads-LLC/open-content-studio`, then
`/plugin install studio@open-content-studio`.

**Content on day one:** install Version 2 and say **"quick start"**. Five questions, then one topic you teach becomes
a carousel, two posts with their graphics and five hooks, each reviewed, in about 20 minutes. No accounts needed
beyond Claude. The craft it follows (content strategy, design, hooks, posts, carousels, reels) ships as playbooks you
can change.

## Yours, start to finish

Everything runs on accounts you open and pay for yourself. Nothing connects back to this repository or to anyone
else's studio, and no one here can see your material, your keys or your posts. Keep your own copy in a private
repository, since it will hold your brand files.
